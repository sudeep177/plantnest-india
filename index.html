Quick care guide

Practical basics for Indian home gardens

🌤️
💧
Water by soil, not the calendar

Check the top 2–3 cm of soil and water when appropriate for your plant. Empty excess water from the saucer.

☀️
Introduce sunlight gradually

Bright indirect light suits many indoor plants. Morning sun is gentler than harsh afternoon sun.

🪴
Use drainage

Choose pots with drainage holes and a suitable, loose potting mix.

🌧️
Adjust for Indian seasons

Hot weather can dry pots quickly. During monsoon, check moisture before watering; growth may slow in cooler months.

🌱
Fertilize lightly

Follow the product label and plant-specific advice. Over-fertilizing can damage roots.
